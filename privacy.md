# Privacy Policy – Rclone S2S Backup

Last updated: September 24, 2026

## Purpose

Rclone S2S Backup is an internal backup solution used by Top Smart Auto to store backup files generated by the company's S2S business application in Google Drive.

## Google User Data

The application uses Google Drive authorization to create, upload, access, and verify backup files associated with the application.

Access to Google Drive is limited to the permissions required for this backup functionality.

## Use of Data

Google user data accessed by the application is used solely for performing and verifying backups.

The application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing.

## Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

## Data Storage

Business backup files are stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the user.

Authentication credentials used by the backup software are stored on the computer running the backup process and are not published on this website.

## Data Retention

Backup files remain in the authorized Google Drive account until they are removed by the account owner or according to the company's backup retention procedures.
